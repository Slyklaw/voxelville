## Objective

Implement a road network with auto-connecting tiles and grid placement rules for buildings.

**Purpose:** Enable roads to connect visually and restrict building placement to road-adjacent cells.
**Output:** Road grid system, adjacency rules, and a demo with roads and buildings placed.
